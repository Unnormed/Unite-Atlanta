■ Why we exist · proposals on the table

Atlanta runs the culture.
Nobody ran its calendar.

Every week this city makes history, and half of it goes unseen. Even by the people making it. Deals close and nobody addresses the community. Festivals vanish and nobody explains why. Events collide because nobody knew.

Unite Atlanta is not another organization. It's the infrastructure the scene never had: one calendar, one archive, one voice. Built by the community it serves, starting with music, starting now.

And it is for all of Atlanta’s music community: hip-hop and R&B, gospel, country, Latin, indie, jazz, classical, and the scenes that don’t have a name yet. If it’s music and it’s Atlanta, it belongs on the calendar.

Mission

We make Atlanta’s music community visible to itself: every event in one place, every win shared, every story preserved.

Vision

A city where the culture is coordinated, its history is protected, and its voice is impossible to ignore.

Positioning

The room where Atlanta’s music community meets itself.

The Room

Unity

One calendar for a scattered city. Promoters, executives, artists and organizers see each other’s moves before they collide, and show up for each other instead of competing on the same night.

“It is by happenstance that most people get together. Let’s be intentional about that.”

The Record

Legacy

Atlanta’s music history is still walking around this city, and nobody is writing it down. We memorialize what happened, document what’s happening, and teach it forward.

“Either we preserve our history, or someone else erases it.”
03

The Voice

Influence

When legislation, elections or industry shifts touch this community, there was never a way to call the room together. An organized Atlanta is an Atlanta that gets heard.

“We must be active participants, not acted upon.”

■ Membership

Open to all.
Plus by invitation.

Unite Atlanta is free and open to everyone: the full public calendar, the journal and the weekly email. Unite Atlanta Plus is the invitation-only side. A code unlocks the Back Room, where private events are listed with their full address and RSVP. The code changes every quarter and is passed on in person, never posted.
