Atlanta Speaks Opinion SEP 18, 2026

The Engineers Already Know Everybody. Ask Us.

Daniel Park
Mix Engineer · Doraville

Here is a normal week in my room. Monday and Tuesday, a trap single for an artist from Zone 6. Wednesday, a forty-voice gospel choir tracked in a church in Lithonia. Thursday through Saturday, a bluegrass record for a band out of Cabbagetown. Three projects, three audiences, three completely separate corners of this city.

Those artists will never meet. They don't play the same rooms or go to the same events, and nothing in their feeds will ever introduce them. The only thing they have in common is the person behind the board. Every engineer in Atlanta can tell you the same story. We are the most connected people in this city's music business and nobody has ever asked us to connect anything.

That is a waste. The choir director needed a horn section last month; the trap producer I worked with on Monday has one on speed dial. The bluegrass band wants to learn how publishing actually works; there is a panel on exactly that happening four miles from their rehearsal space. I know both facts. There is no place for me to put them.

So I'll use this one. The Engineers' Roundtable is on the calendar for the end of October. Come if you record, mix or master here. Come if you have ever paid one of us. And if you run a session, a workshop or a listening night, list it. The city's best network is sitting in dark rooms with headphones on. Give us somewhere to post.
